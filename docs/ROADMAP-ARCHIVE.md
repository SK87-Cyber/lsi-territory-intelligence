### Aug 31, 2026 (Build 1.6)

### Aug 31, 2026 (Build 1.6b)
- **Lighting is identified structurally, never by keyword.** An item counts as Division 14 when its pay-item code falls in the 5000–5299 family or its spec section is 1400–1413. Two keyword false positives proved the point and are kept as regression cases: `SUPPORT, BREAKAWAY STL BM` (S903, a sign support) and `SUPPORTS, OMNI-DIRECTIONAL BREAKAWAY` (4360000000-N, signing). A word search would have published $14,408 of sign hardware as lighting on C205195 and invented a $2,800 lighting contract at C205160.
- **New class `LUMP`** for a bid never broken into items — a design-build lump sum carrying ≥ 50% of the bid. It is not `None` (nothing was read) and not `L3` (which asserts lighting scope *is* confirmed). Design-build contracts are a structural blind spot: lighting there needs the plans or the design-build submittals, and the tabulation will never show it. C205201 was corrected from L3 to LUMP.
- **Completeness test for an item read:** each contract's item rows, summed by bidder, must equal the Board's Item C contract total. All 25 contracts match to the cent, which is what proves no item list was truncated. This check belongs in every backfill run.
- The letting spreadsheet carries itemised prices for a *subset* of bidders (1–4 of as many as 11), always including the low bidder. Contract totals and bid counts still come from Item C; a lighting range quoted "across bidders" from the spreadsheet alone is narrower than the true range, so C205195 keeps the four-bidder range read from the PDF.
- Sample result recorded: across 25 contracts and $252M of low bids from June to August 2026, **one contract carries permanent roadway lighting**. That is the base rate the eight-state model should expect, and the argument for the pipeline (L4) and design-build evidence paths mattering more than letting volume.

### Aug 31, 2026 (Build 1.6c)
- **The STIP is the pipeline source; Board actions are the delta.** The adopted STIP is a queryable map service, so the whole programmed pipeline can be pulled in one query instead of assembled one Board meeting at a time. HL-0154 is funded by the Board but absent from the June 2026 STIP snapshot, which fixes the pattern: query the STIP for the base, then read Board actions forward from the snapshot date for what has been added since.
- Pipeline records carry their **total programmed project cost**, labelled as scope cost, never as a lighting value. L4 stays counted, never valued. The tile reads "total scope, not lighting" for exactly this reason.
- Pipeline projects are sorted by what lighting *is* in them: lighting is the scope, lighting named in scope, or crosswalk beacon lighting. The crosswalk beacons ($35k–$65k RRFB projects) are real but a different product conversation from a high-mast interchange, and lumping them together would flatter the pipeline.
- Third confirmation that `HL` is not a lighting prefix: 66 HL projects in the STIP, almost all intersection and widening work. HL-0065 is C205219, read as No across 245 items. The prefix is retired as a signal.
